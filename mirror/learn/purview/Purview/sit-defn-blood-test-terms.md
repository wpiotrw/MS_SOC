# Blood test terms entity definition | Microsoft Learn

This unbundled named entity detects terms related to blood tests, such as *hCG*. It supports English terms only. It's also included in the [All medical terms and conditions](sit-defn-all-medical-terms-conditions) bundled named entity SIT.

## Confidence level

High

\***UNKNOWN**
