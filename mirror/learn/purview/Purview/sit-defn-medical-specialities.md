# Medical Specialities entity definition | Microsoft Learn

This unbundled named entity detects terms related to medical specialties, such as *dermatology*. It supports English terms only. It's also included in the [All medical terms and conditions](sit-defn-all-medical-terms-conditions) bundled named entity SIT.

## Confidence level

High

## Keyword Highlighting

Supported

When keyword highlighting is supported in the contextual summary for a sensitive information type or a trainable classifier, in the **Contextual Summary** view of activity explorer, the keywords in a document that were matched to a policy are highlighted.
