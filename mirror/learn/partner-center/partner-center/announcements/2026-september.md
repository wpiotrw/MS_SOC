## Reminder: Check Inventory API retiring September 25, 2026

*Reminder: A new Check Inventory by Resource Type API is now available. The previous Check Inventory API will retire on September 25, 2026.*

- **Date**: September 18, 2026
- **Workspace**: APIs
- **Impacted audience**: All partners using Partner Center APIs for inventory checks.

As [previously announced](2026-june#new-check-inventory-by-resource-type-api-availablecheck-inventory-api-retiring-september-25-2026), a new **Check Inventory by Resource Type** API is now available, enabling partners to check inventory for catalog items filtered by a specific resource type. Microsoft introduced this new API to handle the growing complexity of Azure services, where availability also depends on resource type. By requiring a `resourceType` parameter, it enables more scalable and performant inventory checks to address data growth challenges without changing the API response contract. This API replaces the existing Check Inventory API, which retires on **September 25, 2026**.

## Office LTSC 2021 end of support

*Office LTSC 2021, Project LTSC 2021, and Visio LTSC 2021 nearing end of support.*

- **Date**: September 15, 2026
- **Workspace**: General
- **Impacted audience**: All partners, including Cloud Solution Provider (CSP) partners and advisors

Support for Office Long Term Servicing Channel (LTSC) 2021, Project LTSC 2021, and Visio LTSC 2021 ends on October 13, 2026. After this date, these products no longer receive updates, security fixes, or technical support.

These products have served organizations for many years, particularly in specialized or regulated environments. However, continuing to use unsupported versions after end of support might expose organizations to security vulnerabilities, compliance risks, and reduced reliability due to the absence of future updates or security patches.

This end-of-support milestone presents an opportunity for partners to help customers modernize their productivity environments by transitioning to cloud-based alternatives.

#### Recommended upgrade paths

For most customers, Microsoft 365 provides key benefits, including:

- **Up-to-date experiences**, with continuous security updates, feature enhancements, and innovation
- Enterprise-grade **security and management**, designed to support Zero Trust architectures and flexible work scenarios
- **Scalable and flexible licensing**, allowing customers to adjust services as business needs evolve

Customers who move to Microsoft 365 suites gain access to cloud-backed desktop versions of familiar apps such as Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, along with web and mobile access across multiple devices.

- **Enterprise customers** should turn to [Microsoft 365 E3](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/enterprise/e3) as the primary recommended path, with [Office 365 E3](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/enterprise/office-365-e3) or [Microsoft 365 Apps for enterprise](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/enterprise/microsoft-365-apps-for-enterprise-product) used as appropriate fallback options based on customer needs.
- **Businesses with fewer than 300 users** should turn to [Microsoft 365 Business Premium](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-business-premium), [Microsoft 365 Business Standard](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-business-standard), or [Microsoft 365 Apps for business](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-apps-for-business) as supported upgrade paths.
- [Planner and Project Plan 3](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/planner/project-plan-3?msockid=0d135eacd9ed6f1b20b0499fd85e6e52&amp;activetab=pivot:overviewtab) and [Visio Plan 2](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/visio/visio-plan-2?msockid=0d135eacd9ed6f1b20b0499fd85e6e52&amp;activetab=pivot:overviewtab) provide the cloud-based replacements for Project LTSC and Visio LTSC and can be layered on top of Microsoft 365 suites as needed.

Partners should note that Microsoft 365 Copilot is supported only on cloud-backed apps included in Microsoft 365 suites, and on-premises versions of Office aren't eligible. In addition, the Azure Information Protection (AIP) labeling client for perpetual Office has been retired.

In cases where regulatory, connectivity, or technological constraints prevent the use of cloud solutions, customers might consider the latest supported on-premises alternatives, including [Office LTSC 2024](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/enterprise/microsoft-office-volume-licensing-suites-comparison), [Project LTSC 2024](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/p/project-professional-2024/cfq7ttc0ph40), and [Visio LTSC 2024](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/p/visio-professional-2024/CFQ7TTC0PRMB).

#### Next steps

Partners are encouraged to take the following actions:

- **Engage early** with customers still using Office LTSC 2021, Project LTSC 2021, or Visio LTSC 2021 to communicate the October 13, 2026, end-of-support date and associated risks.
- **Prioritize Microsoft 365 E3** when guiding customers toward supported alternatives, with Office 365 E3 or Microsoft 365 Apps for enterprise as appropriate fallback options.
- **Assess customer constraints** related to connectivity, regulation, or offline requirements to determine the most appropriate support path.
- **Leverage available resources** to address common concerns related to cost, migration complexity, or limited usage scenarios.

Partners should begin proactive outreach now to ensure customers have sufficient time to plan and transition before support ends.

## Automatic onboarding for Microsoft Sentinel

*Microsoft Sentinel automatic onboarding for managed customers begins in September 2026.*

- **Date**: September 9, 2026
- **Workspace**: General
- **Impacted audience**: Managed security service providers (MSSPs) and partners managing Microsoft Sentinel customers

Starting in September 2026, Microsoft will begin automatically onboarding managed customers that didn't move to the unified Microsoft Sentinel experience in the Microsoft Defender portal.

Microsoft will manage the scheduling, customer notifications, and technical onboarding. Affected tenant administrators receive an in-portal banner and admin email about 30 days before their onboarding date. Existing third-party connectors, content, and data remain in place.

#### What this change means for you

To prepare for this change, review customers with multiple Microsoft Sentinel workspaces and consider the following information:

- Microsoft selects one primary workspace for Microsoft Defender XDR alerts and incidents.
- XDR-dependent content in secondary workspaces might stop functioning after the primary workspace is established.
- To maintain content from secondary workspaces, migrate affected analytics rules, automation rules, playbooks, and workbooks to the primary workspace.
- For special handling or to request a different primary workspace, email MSSP-Sentinel-Support@microsoft.com with the tenant ID and preferred workspace resource ID.

No action is required for other managed customers.

As a related reminder, the Microsoft Sentinel experience in the Azure portal retires on March 31, 2027.

#### Next steps

Learn more about [Microsoft Sentinel in the Microsoft Defender portal](/en-us/azure/sentinel/workspaces-defender-portal#primary-and-secondary-workspaces?wt.mc_id=xd8rkchdwz).

## New CSP sandbox offer construct preview

*Prepare for the shift to consumption-based offers: Validate the flex spend plan in the sandbox environment starting September 1, 2026.*

- **Date**: September 2, 2026
- **Workspace**: General
- **Impacted audience**: All CSP segments (direct bill, indirect resellers, distributors) and SSPs, as well as SDCs and GSIs/SIs preparing to integrate with the flex spend plan.

Microsoft is continuing to evolve its business toward consumption-based offers, and Cloud Solution Provider (CSP) partners play an important role in that future. On September 1, Microsoft will make [a storage and database test product](https://aka.ms/new-offer-construct-FAQ), flex spend plan, available in the sandbox environment.

CSP partners can then integrate with and validate a flex spend plan. While this test product doesn't represent a final production offer, Microsoft plans to use this same construct for several other consumption products in the future.

The flex spend plan is designed to support capabilities such as a single agreement that can cover multiple eligible products or services, monthly billing rather than full upfront prepayment, coverage across the full-year commitment period, and auto-renewal.

The sandbox test product might not represent the final production offerings. Its purpose is to help partners prepare for future consumption products that use the same construct and strengthen their readiness for the continued shift toward consumption. Note: trials, promotions, and related incentive structures for the new offer type won't be available with the September 1 sandbox test product.

#### Next steps

Use the sandbox offer now to build readiness:

1. Integrate with the new offer type.
2. Test and validate the purchase, billing, and management experience.
3. Identify any required system or operational changes.
4. Prepare for future production offers built on the new construct.

[Learn more in the FAQ](https://aka.ms/new-offer-construct-FAQ) and the Microsoft Learn Document.

## Purchase order mapping in Microsoft Marketplace

*By connecting Microsoft Marketplace purchases to purchase orders, customers can better manage spend while partners unlock more customer opportunities. Purchase order mapping empowers customers to allocate their Marketplace spend, helping them buy with confidence while partners accelerate growth.*

- **Date**: September 1, 2026
- **Workspace**: General
- **Impacted audience**: Any partner with a Marketplace listing.

Microsoft is making it easier for customers to buy, manage, and scale solutions through Microsoft Marketplace. Purchase order mapping helps organizations allocate Marketplace purchases with existing procurement and financial processes.

For partners, this means access to customers who require their cloud and AI spending to align with their purchase orders. By helping organizations fit Marketplace purchases into established business workflows, partners can simplify purchasing conversations and support faster, more confident buying decisions.

As customers expand their Marketplace investments, purchase order mapping helps reinforce trust in the purchasing process while supporting the controls organizations expect. When Marketplace spend is aligned to purchase orders, customers can allocate costs to specific business needs and connect to their procurement systems. This can support a smoother path to adoption, increased Marketplace engagement, and greater opportunities for partner growth.
