# Update your admin phone number and email address - Microsoft 365 admin | Microsoft Learn

This article describes how to update the phone number and email address for yourself as an administrator in Microsoft 365.

- If you're looking for information about how to change your company's name, address, phone number, or technical contact information, see [Change your organization's address, technical contact email, and other information](change-address-contact-and-more).
- For more information about changing user contact information or removing former users, see the Related content section.

## Update your phone number

1. Go to the [Microsoft 365 admin center](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/p/?linkid=2024339) and sign in.
2. Go to **Users** &gt; **Active users**.
3. Select your admin account and choose **Manage contact information** under **Contact information**.
4. Update your phone number and when you're finished, select **Save changes**.

## Update your email address

1. Go to the [Microsoft 365 admin center](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/p/?linkid=2024339) and sign in.
2. Go to **Users** &gt; **Active users**.
3. Select your admin account and choose **Manage username and email** under **Username and email**.
4. Update your email address and when you're finished, select **Save changes**.
